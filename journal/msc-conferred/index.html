MSc conferred, with Distinction

By Jesun Ahmad UshnoPublished 21 Jun 20261 min read
In shortThe MSc in Data Analytics from the University of Niagara Falls Canada was conferred with Distinction, CGPA 3.9154.

The University of Niagara Falls Canada conferred the MSc in Data Analytics on 21 June 2026. The degree carries Distinction. The final CGPA is 3.9154.

The program ran from January 2025 to June 2026. It was the first chapter in Canada, after a BSc in Computer Engineering at AIUB and two years in IT Advisory at KPMG Bangladesh.
