Privacy note

Contact form. It collects your name, email, the mandate type, budget band, timeline and your message. They are stored in the site's own Cloudflare D1 database and forwarded to Jesun's inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). They are kept until he deletes them, and deleted on request. Messages are kept in his inbox for as long as the conversation needs, and deleted on request.

Analytics. None is active. If it is turned on, it will be Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, no fingerprinting, page views only.

Guestbook. Notes you sign are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database with your chosen name and the time. No email, no IP address. Ask and a note is removed.

Visit counter. One visit per browser per day is counted using a salted hash that changes daily. The hash cannot be turned back into an IP address and is not linked to you.

Local storage. Your browser keeps a few small settings on your device only: whether you have seen the build animation, the grid overlay, reduced motion, your arcade scores, and, for this tab only, your recent Cortex conversation. None of it leaves your device unless you send a question to Cortex.

Cortex. When you ask Cortex a question, the question is sent to this site's own server, which looks up passages from this site and, when switched on, asks a small language model on Cloudflare Workers AI to write the answer from those passages. Questions are not stored. Only an anonymous daily counter is kept, to stay inside the free limit.

Everything else. No ads, no trackers. Fonts are served from this site, with no request to any font service. The Lab demos run in your browser and send nothing.

Every number on this site carries a source. If you find an error, email jesunahmadushno@gmail.com with the page and the correct source. Corrections are made in the open and noted on the page with the date.
