FAQ / answered from the record

Questions people ask.

Who is Jesun Ahmad Ushno?

Jesun Ahmad Ushno is a Toronto-based software engineer who builds AI and data systems, agent fleets and machine learning products. He is the Founder of Immitly and Co-Founder of Brimwood Innovation and Climeam. He holds an MSc in Data Analytics with Distinction from the University of Niagara Falls Canada and has published two machine learning papers.

What does Jesun Ahmad Ushno do?

He builds production AI systems: machine learning products, retrieval-augmented generation and multi-agent fleets that run like a company, with roles, seats and jobs. He also runs three ventures: Immitly as Founder, and Brimwood Innovation and Climeam as Co-Founder.

What ventures has Jesun founded?

Immitly, an immigration guidance platform, which he founded. Brimwood Innovation, a Toronto innovation hub he co-founded with Adam Sayani. Climeam, an AI-native creative studio he co-founded and whose work he directs.

What has Jesun published?

Two machine learning papers in AIP Conference Proceedings (2023): one on fabric defect detection with the wavelet transform and a neural network (DOI 10.1063/5.0148618), one on an elitist particle swarm optimizer with mutation and adaptive inertia (DOI 10.1063/5.0148658). He also wrote a book on data analytics (2025) and essays on Medium.

Where is Jesun Ahmad Ushno based?

Toronto, Ontario, Canada. He is from Bangladesh, where he studied computer engineering at AIUB and worked in IT Advisory at KPMG Bangladesh before moving to Canada for his MSc.
