How far can we trust the judge?

Synced from GitHub on November 14, 2026. Verdicts are copied from his roadmap, never edited here.

SummaryJesun Ahmad Ushno runs 58 public studies under one written standard, Standard v2, adopted 2026-11-08. Each study states its question first. A five-rung verdict ladder grades it: Draft, Proposed, Functional, Evaluated, v1.0 Released. 49 studies carry a verdict today, 20 locked their plan before seeing data, and 59 repositories publish a live dashboard. The research spine is one question: how far can an automated judge be trusted?
