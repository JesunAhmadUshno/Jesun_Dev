Frequently asked questions

What does the Google Knowledge Panel service include?

The service includes an entity audit, fact consistency fixes, Knowledge Panel placement through a partner network, and claim help.

How much does the Google Knowledge Panel service cost?

Personal Authority is USD 1,095. Business Authority is USD 1,995. Dual Authority is USD 2,195.

Is a Google Knowledge Panel guaranteed?

Google makes the final decision on each panel. The guarantee is a money guarantee on the placement fee: if no panel is delivered in the window, the placement fee is refunded in full.

Who provides the Google Knowledge Panel service?

Jesun.Ai, a Toronto-based AI company founded by Jesun Ahmad Ushno.
