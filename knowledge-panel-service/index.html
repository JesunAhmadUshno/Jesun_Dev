10FAQ

Can you guarantee the panel?

No. And any seller who says yes is misleading you. Google decides. What we guarantee is the money: no panel inside the window means the placement fee comes back to you.

Why not buy it cheaper direct?

You can buy a bare placement. A bare placement does not include the audit, the fact fixes, the claim help, or one contact who owns the whole process. Most failed panels fail on facts that do not agree. That fix work is the part we do.

It is too expensive.

Compare the price with one lost client. That client searched your name, saw no panel, and picked a rival who looked set up and trusted. If the time is wrong, start with the Entity Foundation Audit at USD 295. We credit it toward the panel if you buy in 30 days.

How long, exactly?

The supplier window is 4 to 10 weeks. We send one update each week. After week 10 you choose: keep waiting, or take the placement refund. No honest seller can give you an exact date, because Google sets the timing.

What do you need from me?

Three things: true facts, one written fact approval in 3 days, and patience for the 4 to 10 week window with no fixed date.

What if my facts are wrong now?

Then we fix them first. Wrong or split facts are the top cause of failed panels. If the fix work is large, we sell you the Entity Foundation Audit first and the panel second.

When do you place my order?

Only after two things: your payment has cleared, and you have approved the fact sheet in writing. No exceptions.

Who owns the panel when it goes live?

You do. We help you claim it in your own Google account. We keep no hold on your panel.

Which panel do you want?

Q1. Does a search for your exact name or company name already show consistent results: a website, social profiles and at least some third-party mentions that agree with each other?

Help: search your exact name in Google in a private window. Look at the first page.

Q2. Do you have at least one independent source about you that you did not write yourself: press, a podcast, a book listing, a company registry or an institutional profile?

Help: a source you did not write means a page on a site you do not own or control.

Q3. Are your name, photo, role and location the same on every public profile, word for word?

Help: check your site, LinkedIn, and your main social profile side by side.

Q4. For a business panel: is the company registered, with a real address, a website and matching listings?

Help: matching listings means the same name, address, and phone in each place.

Q5. Can you approve a one-page fact sheet in writing within 3 days, and wait 4 to 10 weeks without a guaranteed date?

Help: the fact sheet is one page. Your written approval can be a clear email reply.
