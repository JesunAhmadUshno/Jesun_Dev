About Jesun Ahmad Ushno

Jesun Ahmad Ushno is a Toronto based AI Engineer and Data Specialist at Gallea Ai, where he works on production retrieval-augmented generation and multi-agent orchestration. He is a published machine learning researcher, the founder and CEO of Jesun.Ai, and the creator of Jesun.Code, a plain-English programming language. He holds an MSc in Data Analytics from the University of Niagara Falls Canada, where he graduated with distinction in 2026, and a bachelor's degree from American International University-Bangladesh. Before Gallea, he worked in IT Advisory at KPMG Bangladesh, rising from Officer to Consultant.
