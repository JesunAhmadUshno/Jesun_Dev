From January to March 2026 the work was dynamic feature extraction for drawing-based measurement at mentAImage.

The pipeline reads how a person draws: coordinates, time and pressure. It produces 32 metrics per session from seven drawing tasks. 35 sessions were analysed. Ten indicators map to stress, anxiety, burnout and cognitive load scores.

It is a research build, not a clinical product. The validation study is the open work.

Code: [drawing-modality-test on GitHub](https://github.com/JesunAhmadUshno/drawing-modality-test). Case file: [PulseKey](/projects/pulsekey.html).
