Systems wall / four live demos

His methods, runnable.

Two are his published papers. Two are the principles behind his products. Each states what it is and what it is not.

Wavelet defect detection / paper 2023Paper
RawDetected

A synthetic woven texture, generated in the browser. The wavelet step of the method only: a 3-level Haar transform averages out the weave, then blocks that deviate from the fabric are flagged. The paper adds a neural network classifier.

Elitist PSO / paper 2023Paper

30 particles on the 2D Rastrigin function, a standard test with many local minima. Same seed for both modes, so you can compare them.

Fleet routing / how he runs agentsSeats

A simulation of the routing idea: each task goes to an idle seat with the matching job. Seat names come from his fleet. Numbers are simulated, not production metrics.
